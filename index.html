ABOUT S.WORDS

Where words find rhythm and music finds its voice.

S.WORDS is the Rap & Indie Music Club of Shoolini University, built for artists, writers, performers, producers, and music lovers.

We bring together people who love creating and experiencing music through rap, indie, songwriting, beat-making, cyphers, and live performances.

From original compositions to collaborations and stage performances, S.WORDS is a space to express, experiment, connect, and create.

EVENTS & ACTIVITIES

S.WORDS is more than a music club. It is a space to learn, experiment, perform and grow as an artist.

01

MUSIC PRODUCTION

Learn the fundamentals of music production and explore how ideas turn into complete musical pieces.

02

ARTIST DEVELOPMENT

Build your identity as an artist through creative exploration, performance skills, collaboration and finding your own sound.

03

LYRICISM

Develop your writing and storytelling skills through lyric-writing sessions, creative exercises and collaborative writing.

04

CYPHERS

Step into the cypher, express yourself and become comfortable performing around others while building confidence and stage presence.

05

LIVE PERFORMANCES

Get opportunities to perform at university events, club showcases and other platforms, turning practice into real stage experience.

06

ARTIST OPPORTUNITIES

We connect our artists with performance opportunities on platforms such as Spotlight and other stages, helping them take their music beyond the campus.
